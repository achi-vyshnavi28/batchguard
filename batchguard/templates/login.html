BatchGuard

When a pharma plant makes a batch of tablets, every step must be recorded, checked by a second person and signed before the batch can be released. BatchGuard does this electronically, following the FDA's rules for electronic records (21 CFR Part 11) and data integrity (ALCOA+).

Can't be fakedEvery change goes into a tamper-evident audit trail; nothing is ever overwritten.
Can't skip checksA batch is blocked from release until every step is verified and every problem is closed.
